# Factions ship as archetypes; Standing is tiered and the DM confirms changes

Grimoire tracks how Factions regard the party so that social checks, Shop prices, NPC first reactions and Encounter Tables can follow from it. The well-known named factions of published settings are not in the SRD, so Grimoire cannot ship them. It ships a catalogue of generic Faction Archetypes that DMs copy and name, plus the Default World's own Factions.

Standing is kept as five tiers (Hostile, Unfriendly, Neutral, Friendly, Allied) rather than a visible score. It is tracked for the party, and a character may carry a Personal Standing that overrides it. Grimoire suggests Standing Changes from events; the DM confirms or edits each one and decides whether Players see the reason. Players see tiers, never numbers.

## Considered options

- Automatic, visible scores: transparent, but every kill or bargain would move numbers the DM did not choose, and players would optimise the number instead of the relationship.
- Per-character standing only: precise, but most tables think of "the party" as the one the Faction knows; overrides cover the exceptions.

## Consequences

The rules engine reads one tier per roll: advantage on social checks with members when Allied, disadvantage when Hostile, and a small bonus or penalty in between. Shops in the Faction apply a price modifier per tier, and Encounter Tables in its territory weight its entries by tier. MCP tools can propose Standing Changes, but those stay pending until the DM confirms them.

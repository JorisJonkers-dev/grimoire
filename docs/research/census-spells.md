# Census: the Effect component kinds every SRD 5.2 spell needs

Research for [#237](https://github.com/JorisJonkers-dev/grimoire/issues/237), part of the map in [#236](https://github.com/JorisJonkers-dev/grimoire/issues/236). This file is written by [`census-spells.py`](census-spells.py) from the Compendium seed at commit `92a4a7a`. Change the script and run it again; do not edit this file by hand.

## The answer in short

- The seed holds **339** SRD 5.2 spells. Every one has a row in [the table](#the-table).
- **12** can be stated in full with the kinds the engine has today. For 10 of them the Scaling is expressible too.
- **40** are proposed for the Manual exception list, because what they produce is something only a person at the table can supply.
- **287** are gaps: they need at least one of **36** kinds the engine lacks, or one of **11** kinds it has in too narrow a form.
- The kinds most spells wait on are `save_gate` (58), `timing` (50), `grant_action` (39), `damage` (37), `roll_modifier` (34), `create_object` (27).
- Built in the order suggested below, the first five kinds finish 35 of the gap spells, the first ten finish 70 and the first twenty finish 147. All 47 are needed to finish every one.
- Scaling: of the 299 spells outside the exception list, 183 do not grow, 63 grow by dice in a way the existing axes express, and 53 grow something the Scaling record cannot hold.

## What the engine has today

The stored constraint lists 29 names. One of them, `mode`, is not a component kind: it is the row a `choice` keeps for each of its modes. The Go union has 30 types: the other 28 stored kinds, plus `area_save` and `light`. Those two are built by the Homebrew spell builder and run in play, but have no catalogue table, so a seeded SRD Effect cannot hold them yet. This census counts all 30 as existing and marks the two.

| Kind | What it states today |
|---|---|
| `area` | the template: shape, size, range |
| `area_save` | the save every creature in an area makes (builder only, no catalogue table) |
| `save_damage` | damage to every creature in the area, halved or avoided on a save |
| `save_condition` | a condition on every creature in the area that fails the save |
| `create_surface` | a Surface on the area for some rounds |

| `bonus_die` | a die added to attack rolls, saving throws, or both |

| `save_edge` | advantage, disadvantage or sure failure on saves of one ability |
| `extra_damage` | extra dice when the Effect's source hits the bearer |
| `move_cost` | a multiplier on what each foot of movement costs |
| `speed_penalty` | feet taken off the bearer's Speed |
| `crit_within` | hits from within some feet are Critical Hits |
| `incapacitated` | no actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions |
| `immobile` | Speed held at 0 |

| `reacts` | a Reaction on a trigger, resolved by the DM from an instruction |

| `teleport` | the caster teleports to an open hex within range |
| `dispel` | every spell on the bearer ends |

| `grant_feature` | a named feature, noted to the DM |
| `resource_change` | uses of a Resource given back or taken |
| `summon` | a fixed count of one monster under the caster's control |
| `form` | the bearer takes a creature's stat block |
| `choice` | modes; whoever applies the Effect picks one |
| `branch` | extra parts when a save fails by N, Hit Points are at most N, on the first touch in a turn, or for a creature type |
| `manual` | a part the DM resolves from a prompt |

Limits of these kinds that decided rows below, each read from the code:

- A saving throw is rolled only by creatures inside an `area`. An Effect put on a chosen creature lands with no save; only its repeat save at the end of a turn is rolled (`planApply` and `saves` in `play/live/effects.go`).
- No kind rolls a spell attack, restores Hit Points, or changes Armor Class.
- An `area` touches every creature in it. Nothing spares the caster's allies or caps the number of targets.
- Later timings exist only through a Surface: save-less damage or an Effect on a creature that enters it or starts its turn there (`rules/surface/surface.go`).
- `reacts` and `grant_feature` both end in a sentence for the DM (`ReactionsTo`, and `land` in `play/live/effects.go`), so neither computes an outcome.
- Scaling grows one dice expression. In play it is applied only on the `slot_level` axis and only to an area's `save_damage` (`upcast` in `play/live/area.go`); `character_level` steps are stored and written as rules text but no cast applies them.
- The Design in `spellbuild.go` always has an area, takes one save, and offers six part kinds: damage, condition, light, reveal, surface, manual.

## How the rows were judged

The script makes a first pass from the structured fields and the description, and trusts itself only when a spell matches a plain pattern with no rider words. It was sure of 15 spells. Then all 339 descriptions were read and classified by hand; the hand rows are the census and sit in the script as data. Where the first pass was sure, it agrees with the hand row for 15 of 15 spells; its Scaling guess agrees for 298 of the 298 spells it was sure of. Run the script with `--diff` to list the rest.

The bar for a row:

- **Needed** is every kind an Effect must hold so that the rules text generated from it says what the SRD description says. A kind is listed once however often the spell uses it.
- **Missing** is the subset the engine cannot state today: a kind it lacks, or one it has in too narrow a form, written with a `+`.
- A rule that changes what happens to a creature counts, however small: a creature type that saves with Disadvantage, a spell that ends when its target takes damage.
- Four things do not count: what happens to unattended objects (Fireball lighting what is flammable), how far a noise carries, wind dispersing a cloud, and a sentence that only describes (Cone of Cold's frozen statue). The seeded Fireball, Thunderwave and Cone of Cold set that precedent.
- Putting an Effect on a chosen, willing creature counts as possible today, because that is how the seeded Bless works: it is applied to each token. Range, sight and the number of targets are not checked. 177 of the spells outside the exception list are cast on chosen creatures, objects or points rather than on the caster or an area, so this is the largest thing the census takes on trust; see [Not verified](#not-verified).
- **Scaling** is `-` when the spell does not grow, `yes` when it grows by dice per slot level or by dice steps at character levels, and `no` with what grows when it is something else.
- **Exception** is yes only when the spell's outcome is content or a ruling that a person at the table has to supply. A spell that is hard to model is a gap.

### A build order

Most gap spells wait on several kinds at once, so the ranking above overstates what one kind buys. It counts spells and nothing else: not how often a spell is cast at a table, and not what a kind costs to build. It is one input to the gap-ordering decision, not that decision.

### Scaling

63 spells grow by dice on the `slot_level` axis or by dice steps on `character_level`, which the Scaling record holds. 53 grow something else. The axes are not what is missing: every one of these grows with the slot or the caster's level. What is missing is the quantity. The record holds dice and nothing but dice.

| What grows | Spells |
|---|---:|
| number of targets | 21 |
| duration | 8 |
| summoned creatures: count or stat block | 6 |
| a flat number | 4 |
| area or amount | 4 |
| number of beams, darts, rays or leaps | 4 |
| a spell-level threshold | 3 |
| two rolls by different dice | 2 |
| range | 1 |

Three notes on the `yes` rows. A cast applies Scaling only to an area's `save_damage` today, so healing dice and attack damage dice grow in the data but not in play until `heal` and `damage` exist. The thirteen cantrips that step at levels 5, 11 and 17 use the `character_level` axis, which no cast applies yet. And where one step grows two damage rolls by the same die (Acid Arrow, Flame Strike) the row says `yes`, on the reading that one Scaling can be applied to both.

### Proposed Manual exceptions

40 spells, in five groups. The first four are DM judgment by nature. The fifth is a different case and should be decided as a group.

**The DM supplies the knowledge** (13)

- `augury`: the DM chooses the omen from what will happen in the story
- `commune`: the DM answers the questions as the deity
- `commune-with-nature`: the DM supplies facts about the surrounding region
- `contact-other-plane`: the DM answers the questions as the contacted entity
- `detect-thoughts`: the DM says what the target is thinking
- `divination`: the DM gives the reply
- `find-the-path`: the route through the world is the DM's to describe
- `legend-lore`: the DM supplies the lore
- `locate-animals-or-plants`: whether and where such a creature or plant exists within 5 miles is the DM's to say
- `scrying`: what the sensor shows of a distant creature or place is the DM's to describe
- `speak-with-animals`: what the Beast knows and says is the DM's to voice
- `speak-with-dead`: what the corpse knows and says is the DM's to voice
- `speak-with-plants`: what the plants report is the DM's to voice

**The illusion is whatever the caster invents** (8)

- `hallucinatory-terrain`: the look of the false terrain and whether it fools anyone are narrated
- `major-image`: the image is whatever the caster invents and whether it fools anyone is narrated
- `minor-illusion`: the sound or image is whatever the caster invents and whether it fools anyone is narrated
- `mirage-arcane`: the false terrain is whatever the caster invents
- `phantasmal-force`: the phantasm is whatever the caster invents and how the target reacts to it is narrated
- `programmed-illusion`: the scripted illusion and its trigger are whatever the caster describes
- `project-image`: what the copy says and does at a distant place, and who it fools, are narrated
- `silent-image`: the image is whatever the caster invents and whether it fools anyone is narrated

**Free words: an instruction, a bargain or a message** (8)

- `animal-messenger`: a described recipient at a distant place and a free-form message; nothing on the Map to resolve
- `dream`: a conversation and a dreamscape narrated at the table
- `geas`: a free-form command; whether the creature acts against it is the DM's call
- `magic-mouth`: a free-form trigger and a spoken message; no rules outcome
- `mass-suggestion`: a free-form suggestion; how each creature pursues it is narrated
- `modify-memory`: the new memory and what it changes are narrated; the DM may rule it has no effect
- `planar-ally`: which creature answers, whether it serves and at what price are the DM's to decide
- `suggestion`: a free-form suggestion; how the creature pursues it is narrated

**Open-ended creation or wish** (4)

- `creation`: the object is whatever the caster describes, within a size limit
- `fabricate`: the product is whatever the caster designs from the raw materials
- `stone-shape`: the shape is whatever the caster designs, and what it is good for is narrated
- `wish`: reshaping reality is ruled by the DM; the spell can also stand in for any other spell

**No rules outcome to compute** (7)

- `druidcraft`: no rules outcome: harmless sensory effects narrated at the table
- `elementalism`: no rules outcome: harmless elemental effects narrated at the table
- `illusory-script`: no rules outcome: hidden or altered writing narrated at the table
- `mending`: no rules outcome: a small repair narrated at the table
- `prestidigitation`: no rules outcome: minor tricks narrated at the table
- `purify-food-and-drink`: no rules outcome: food and drink made safe, narrated at the table
- `thaumaturgy`: no rules outcome beyond one skill edge: minor wonders narrated at the table

The last group holds spells whose text has nothing for an engine to work out: a flower blooms, a shirt is cleaned. They are not hard and nobody rules on them. They are listed here because an Effect that says nothing would pass as fully automated while proving nothing against the SRD text. The alternative to an exception is to let such an Effect count as full with its description as its only content. That choice belongs to the maintainer.

### Borderline calls

Rows a reasonable reader could put on the other side of the line.

Proposed as exceptions, though part of the spell is computable:

- `detect-thoughts`, `scrying`, `phantasmal-force`, `dream`, `contact-other-plane`, `geas`: each has a save, and some deal damage, around a core the DM narrates.
- `find-the-path`, `locate-animals-or-plants`: the answer is a fact about the world. If the place or creature is on the world map the engine could give it.
- `stone-shape`, `magic-mouth`, `animal-messenger`, `project-image`: a bounded effect whose content the caster makes up.

Counted as gaps, though they lean on the DM:

- `contingency`, `glyph-of-warding`, `symbol`, `imprisonment`, `sequester`: the caster words a trigger. The census assumes a list of typed triggers and leaves the unusual ones to the DM.
- `locate-creature`, `locate-object`, `identify`, `detect-magic`: computable for what is on the Map or in an inventory, a question for the DM otherwise.
- `control-weather`, `control-water`, `move-earth`, `guards-and-wards`, `magnificent-mansion`, `gate`, `awaken`, `planar-binding`, `magic-jar`, `clone`: determinate, but each needs a part of the world the engine may never model.
- `comprehend-languages`, `tongues`, `water-breathing`, `zone-of-truth`, `message`, `sending`, `telepathic-bond`: an outcome with no number in it. They are gaps only if languages, breathing and private channels are things the engine tracks.

## Missing kinds defined

- **`save_gate`** (58). A saving throw made by chosen creatures rather than by everyone in an `area`: the cast names its targets, rolls each save against the spell save DC, and fires the parts under it on a failure or a success. Today a save is rolled only by creatures inside an `area`. (`SaveGate` in ARCHITECTURE.md 9.3.)
- **`timing`** (50). When a part fires after the cast: on entering the area, at the start or end of a turn (the creature's or the caster's), once or every turn, with its own save, and counting successes and failures where the spell asks for three of a kind. Today the only later timing is save-less damage from a Surface.
- **`grant_action`** (39). While the Effect lasts, the caster or bearer gains an action, Bonus Action or Magic action that fires typed parts again, moves the Effect, or switches its mode (Call Lightning, Spiritual Weapon, Moonbeam). `grant_feature` only notes a name to the DM.
- **`damage`** (37). Damage that is not behind an area save: on a hit, on a miss, with no roll to avoid it (Magic Missile), or death outright under a Hit Point threshold. Covers flat modifiers and the spellcasting ability modifier. (`Damage` in 9.3.)
- **`roll_modifier`** (34). Modifiers on D20 Tests that `bonus_die`, `edge` and `save_edge` cannot state: ability checks and Initiative, a subtracted die, a flat number, a replaced roll, and an edge or automatic result that depends on who rolls or attacks (creature type, size, a Sense) or on the circumstance.
- **`create_object`** (27). Places a thing on the Map that is not a creature: a wall, dome, cage, sensor, floating weapon or hand, with a position and where it applies Armor Class, Hit Points, cover and what it blocks. (`CreateObject` in 9.3.)
- **`trigger`** (24). Parts that fire on an event rather than a turn boundary: the bearer hits, is hit, takes damage, drops to 0 Hit Points, is targeted, or the Effect ends. Typed parts, unlike `reacts`, whose outcome is a sentence for the DM. (`Trigger` in 9.3.)
- **`ends_when`** (23). An early end other than a repeat save or lost concentration: the target takes damage, attacks or casts, moves out of range, dons armor, or a counter runs out.
- **`attack_gate`** (21). A melee or ranged spell attack roll against a chosen target, with parts that fire on a hit and optionally on a miss. (`AttackGate` in 9.3.)
- **`ward`** (21). An area or bearer that blocks spells of some level, teleportation, planar travel, Divination, sound, or the passage of creatures of stated types.
- **`target_filter`** (17). Targeting that picks: only creatures the caster chooses inside an area, allies only, enemies only, or a cap on how many. Today an `area` touches every creature in it.
- **`compel`** (14). The spell dictates what the target does on its turn: flee, approach, halt, drop what it holds, dance in place, attack at random, or obey the caster outright.
- **`heal`** (14). Hit Points restored: dice plus the spellcasting ability modifier, a fixed amount, all of them, or a pool the caster divides. Also a change to healing the bearer receives. (`Heal` in 9.3.)
- **`resistance`** (14). Resistance, Immunity or Vulnerability to damage types, or damage taken reduced by a die.
- **`alter_map`** (12). Changes Map Objects, terrain or weather: locks and unlocks, opens a passage, reshapes earth or water, destroys an object.
- **`end_effect`** (12). Ends or suppresses named conditions, curses or Effects on the target (Lesser Restoration), as opposed to `dispel`, which ends every spell.
- **`speed_modifier`** (12). A Speed bonus or multiplier, a granted Fly, Climb or Swim Speed, hovering, jumping, or ignoring Difficult Terrain. `speed_penalty` only subtracts feet.
- **`apply_condition`** (11). A condition put on the bearer with no area save: outright (Invisibility), under a `save_gate` or `attack_gate`, or under a Hit Point threshold. (`ApplyCondition` in 9.3.)
- **`check_gate`** (11). An ability check that decides an outcome: the action a Restrained creature takes to break free, the caster's check in Dispel Magic, the Investigation check out of Maze.
- **`action_economy`** (10). Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions or whole turns granted or taken away: Haste's extra action, Slow's limit, Dash as a Bonus Action, no Opportunity Attacks, Time Stop's extra turns.
- **`condition_immunity`** (10). The bearer cannot gain named conditions, from anyone or from stated creature types.
- **`travel`** (10). Moves creatures off the current Map: to a known place, a teleportation circle, another plane, or an extradimensional room.
- **`restriction`** (9). Something the bearer cannot do that is not a condition: regain Hit Points, cast spells, speak or lie, make ranged attacks, be raised as Undead.
- **`sense`** (9). Grants a Sense with a range (Darkvision, Truesight, seeing the Invisible) or sight and hearing through a remote sensor. (`Sense` in 9.3.)
- **`attack_modifier`** (8). A change to the bearer's own attacks: a bonus to hit and damage, extra or fewer damage dice, a new damage die or type, the spellcasting ability in place of Strength. `extra_damage` only covers hits by the Effect's source against the bearer.
- **`banish`** (8). Takes a creature off the Map for a time and puts it back where it left, or sends it to another plane for good.
- **`ac_modifier`** (7). A bonus or penalty to Armor Class, or a new base or floor for it (Shield, Mage Armor, Barkskin).
- **`detect`** (7). Tells the caster which things of a named class are within range: magic, poison, traps, creature types, a known creature or object, an item's properties. `reveal` strips Visibility Qualities for everyone; this informs one creature.
- **`revive`** (7). A dead creature returns to life with stated Hit Points, within a time limit; also making a dying creature Stable.
- **`roll_table`** (7). Rolls a die on a table and applies the row's parts (Confusion, Prismatic Spray, Reincarnate, Teleport).
- **`stat_modifier`** (7). Sets or changes a statistic that is not Speed or Armor Class: size, an ability score, creature type, languages understood, breathing water.
- **`create_item`** (6). Creates, conjures, stores or recalls items: berries, food and water, a chest kept on another plane.
- **`create_trap`** (5). Places a Trap or stored spell that fires its Effect when a trigger area is entered or touched.
- **`grant_quality`** (5). Gives the bearer a Visibility Quality other than Invisible: Disguised with a shown identity, Ethereal, or Hidden.
- **`hp_max`** (3). Raises or lowers the Hit Point maximum.
- **`message`** (3). Opens a private channel between creatures regardless of distance.

## Existing kinds that need widening

- **`light+`** (13). Light that travels with the creature or object that bears it, dim light alone, and sunlight.
- **`area+`** (11). An area that moves after the cast (with the caster's action, on its own each turn, or with the object it is cast on), or several areas in one cast (Meteor Swarm's four spheres, Fire Storm's ten cubes).
- **`summon+`** (10). A stat block chosen from a list at cast, a count or statistics that grow with the slot, a creature made from a corpse or object, a control period that must be renewed.
- **`forced_move+`** (8). Movement in a direction the caster picks, upward, or by a distance worked out from an ability modifier. Today it is straight away from or toward the origin.
- **`save_damage+`** (6). More than one damage roll or type under one save (Flame Strike), or a flat number added to or in place of the dice (Disintegrate's 10d6 + 40, Guardian of Faith's 20).
- **`form+`** (5). Limits on the form (creature type, Challenge Rating against the target's level), what the bearer keeps, and forms that are objects.
- **`dispel+`** (4). Ending only spells up to a level, with a check for higher ones, or only spells of one kind (light, darkness).
- **`teleport+`** (3). Teleporting someone or something other than the caster, bringing a creature along, a destination the caster cannot see, and repeated use while the Effect lasts.
- **`create_surface+`** (2). A lightly obscured Surface.
- **`temp_hp+`** (2). Temporary Hit Points from dice or from an ability modifier, and granted again each turn.
- **`resource_change+`** (1). Everything a Short Rest gives back, not one named Resource.

## Seed defects noticed

Found while reading; none is fixed here.

- `description` is empty for `greater-invisibility`. Its row is classified from the SRD 5.2 rule that the target has the Invisible condition for the duration.
- `moonbeam` starts mid-spell, without the sentence that sets the Cylinder; `conjure-celestial` names two lights and describes neither; `telekinesis` stops mid-sentence; `tsunami` ends with a stray section heading.
- `attackRoll` is true for 24 spells whose text makes no spell attack (Bless, Blur, Foresight and others); each of them mentions attack rolls for another reason. `saveAbility` is set for `freedom-of-movement`, whose text has no saving throw. `damageRoll` holds dice that are not damage for several more (Bless 1d4, Blink 1d6, Teleport 1d100). A loader that maps these fields to parts would be wrong for those spells.
- `scaling` has rows for damage dice only. 56 spells have `higherLevel` text and no `scaling` rows.
- `chain-lightning` and `dissonant-whispers` have no `higherLevel` text, and may be missing it; see below.

## Not verified

- The seed was not compared with the SRD 5.2 document itself. Rows follow the seed's text, so a wrong or cut-off description gives a wrong row. The five spells named above are the known cases.
- Whether `chain-lightning` and `dissonant-whispers` grow with the slot in SRD 5.2. Their rows say `-`, as the seed does. The SRD 5.1 Chain Lightning in the same seed adds a bolt per slot level, which suggests the 5.2 entry lost its text. The seed's upstream API could not be reached from where this was written, so neither was checked.
- That applying an Effect to a chosen token is an acceptable stand-in for Targeting of chosen creatures. If it is not, a kind for it comes ahead of everything in the ranking, since it is needed by every spell counted in the trust note above.
- Nothing was run. The limits listed under "What the engine has today" come from reading the code, not from casting a spell in a Session.
- The missing kinds are named and scoped by this census. Whether `timing` and `trigger`, or `save_gate` and `apply_condition`, should be one kind or two is for the engine-gap ticket to settle; merging two rows of the ranking changes the order.

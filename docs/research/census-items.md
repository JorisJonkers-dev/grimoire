# Census: which Effect components SRD 5.2 items with behaviour need

Research for [#240](https://github.com/JorisJonkers-dev/grimoire/issues/240), a ticket on the map
[#236](https://github.com/JorisJonkers-dev/grimoire/issues/236) (full rules automation of SRD 5.2).
Taken against `main` at `92a4a7a` (release 1.3.0) on 2026-10-12.

**Question.** For every SRD 5.2 item with rules behaviour in the Compendium seed, magic or not: which
Item Properties and Effect component kinds does Automation Level full need, which of them does the
engine already have, and which are missing?

The tables on this page are generated by [`census-items.py`](census-items.py) from the seed and from
the classification written in that script. [`census-items.csv`](census-items.csv) maps every seed
slug to its entry. To regenerate both after a change, run `python3 docs/research/census-items.py`
from the repository root.

## Headline numbers

<!-- BEGIN HEADLINE -->
- The seed holds **963** SRD 5.2 items, 760 of them flagged magic.
- **842** have rules behaviour. Grouped, they are **243 entries**: 53 templates that cover 652 seed rows, and 190 single items.
- **121** have no rules behaviour of their own and are excluded.
- **10 entries (15 seed rows)** can be authored with today's Effect component kinds and item mechanics alone.
- The other 233 entries wait on 28 missing Effect component kinds, 8 missing item mechanics and 25 one-off mechanics.
- **28 entries (42 seed rows)** only grant spells; 26 more grant spells beside other behaviour. Between them they name 99 distinct spells, all present in the seed.
- Charges: 42 entries (93 seed rows), plus 19 entries with a single limited use that fits the Charges model. Attunement: 120 entries (385 seed rows). Consumable use: 43 entries (72 seed rows). A curse: 4 entries (28 seed rows).
- **4 entries** are proposed for the Manual exception list, each for part of its behaviour only.
<!-- END HEADLINE -->

The three findings that matter most for ordering the engine work:

1. **Flat bonuses come first.** `modifier` alone completes the +1, +2 and +3 weapons, armor, Shields,
   ammunition and wands and the handful of protection items: more seed rows than any other single
   kind, because those rows are one template repeated over every base weapon and armor.
2. **Magic weapons are one cluster.** Almost every weapon with a rider needs the same four kinds
   together: `modifier`, `trigger` (on a hit, or on a natural 20), `damage` and `save_gate`. None of
   them unlocks much alone; together they unlock most of the weapon rows.
3. **Items mostly lack item mechanics, not exotic Effects.** SRD items carry no Item Property rows
   today, no Item Property turns into an Effect, and a granted spell cannot carry the save DC the item
   states. Those gaps sit under nearly every entry and are listed apart from the Effect component
   kinds below.

## What the engine has today

### Effect component kinds

The Go union in `effects` has 30 concrete component types. In the database's spelling they are:

`bonus_die`, `edge`, `extra_damage`, `move_cost`, `manual`, `area`, `save_damage`, `save_condition`,
`create_surface`, `incapacitated`, `immobile`, `save_edge`, `crit_within`, `exhausting`,
`speed_penalty`, `reacts`, `temp_hp`, `teleport`, `forced_move`, `dispel`, `counter`,
`grant_feature`, `resource_change`, `choice`, `branch`, `summon`, `form`, `reveal`, `area_save`,
`light`.

The kind check in `00077_visibility.sql` accepts 29 strings: the first 28 of those and `mode`, which
is a row of a `choice`. `area_save` and `light` are not in the check: they exist only inside a spell
Design, as the comment in `api/internal/compendium/pgstore/effects.go` says. The map's count of 29
kinds is the database's.

Three properties of these kinds shape the whole census:

- **Saves are area-first.** `area_save`, `save_damage` and `save_condition` act on every creature in
  an `area`, and the DC is always the caster token's spell save DC, or 13 when the stat block has
  none (`spellDC` in `play/live/area.go`). No kind carries a DC of its own.
- **Nothing adds a flat number.** An attack takes bonus dice (`bonus_die`), advantage and
  disadvantage (`edge`) and a penalty from exhaustion. There is no flat bonus to attack rolls, damage
  rolls, Armor Class or saves.
- **`extra_damage` is a mark, not a weapon rider.** It adds dice to hits against the bearer by the
  Effect's source, with no damage type. That is Hunter's Mark, and the Oathbow's sworn enemy; it is
  not a Flame Tongue.

`ARCHITECTURE.md` §9.3 describes a wider union than the code has: `Damage`, `Heal`, `SaveGate`,
`AttackGate`, `ApplyCondition`, `Modifier`, `Sense`, `CreateObject` and `Trigger` are named there and
absent from `effects.go`. Where a missing kind below matches one of those, it uses that name.

Around the components, an Effect has a Duration (instant, rounds, minutes, hours, until dispelled,
until a rest, permanent, end of next turn, until cured, with a repeated save), Scaling (by spell slot,
character level, class level or class table column) and four `branch` conditions (`fails_by`,
`hp_at_most`, `first_each_turn`, `creature_is`). Surfaces are data: a kind has a movement cost, an
obscurement (heavy or darkness), hazard dice and an Effect, and the built-in kinds include fire,
grease, fog and spikes.

### Item Properties

The Homebrew item builder's Design has an enchantment of +0 to +3, an Attunement requirement,
weapon properties with a Weapon Mastery, Charges (a maximum, and dice plus a bonus regained at dawn,
on a Long Rest or on a Short Rest) and up to 40 Item Property rows of 18 types:

`skill_boost`, `bonus`, `resistance`, `extra_damage`, `sense`, `speed`, `cantrip`, `spell`, `light`,
`consumable`, `curse`, `sentient`, `growth`, `set_bonus`, `container`, `firearm`, `trigger`,
`manual`.

Six of them (`curse`, `sentient`, `growth`, `set_bonus`, `trigger`, `manual`) are text: the builder
validates a description and nothing else.

### What play does with an item today

- **SRD items have no Item Property rows.** An SRD item reaches play as its name, weight, category
  and Attunement requirement, plus Charges for the slugs in `compendium.item_charges`
  (`Items` in `play/pgstore/inventory.go`). Its behaviour is its description text.
- **A Homebrew item's rows are read for three things:** its card, its Charges and Attunement, and the
  spells it grants (`homebrewItem` in the same file). No row becomes an Effect; the enchantment and
  the `bonus`, `resistance`, `sense` and `speed` rows change no roll.
- **An Item Instance can be** drunk (a potion; it heals when `inventory.Healing` knows the slug),
  thrown away, attuned (three at most, with the class or spellcaster requirement checked),
  identified, drained of Charges, or cast from. Casting from an item checks the grant, the
  identification and the Attunement and spends the Charges (`CastFrom` in `play/app/inventory.go`);
  the spell then resolves like any other cast, with the caster's own save DC.
- **The three Effects owned by Item Properties are Weapon Mastery effects** (`sapped`, `slowed`,
  `vexed`), seeded in `00061_weapon_mastery.sql`. No magic item owns an Effect.

So two pieces of wiring come before any item below can reach full, whatever kinds it needs. They are
not Effect components and are not counted as missing in the table, because every entry would list
them:

- **Worn Effect.** An Item Property owns an Effect that is active on the bearer while the Item
  Instance is equipped, held or carried, and attuned where it must be. Entries marked `worn` need it.
- **Use action.** An item action with its cost in the action economy (a Magic action, a Bonus Action,
  Utilize, a Reaction, or one attack of the Attack action) that spends Charges or the item itself and
  resolves an owned Effect with Targeting. Entries marked `action` or `consumed` need it.

The map already lists this under "Not yet specified" (how Charges, Attunement and consumable use are
expressed for SRD items).

## Method

1. **Group.** The script matches every seed row to exactly one entry or one exclusion, by slug
   pattern for the templated rows (the same text over each base weapon or armor, or over +1, +2 and
   +3) and by slug for the rest. It fails if a row matches nothing or two things.
2. **First pass.** The script guesses a pattern from the description with a handful of regular
   expressions and guesses the Charges, consumable and curse flags from the wording.
3. **Read by hand.** Every distinct description was then read: the 50 distinct magic weapon and armor
   texts, all 211 other magic items and all 203 nonmagical rows. The final classification is the
   hand one. The script prints where its guessed flags differ from the hand flags; each difference
   was checked.
4. **Check.** The script verifies that every kind, Item Property and pattern named is in its
   vocabulary, that every spell an entry names is a spell in the seed and is named in the entry's
   text, and that every creature an entry summons is a monster in the seed.

<!-- BEGIN FIRSTPASS -->
The first pass guessed a pattern for 663 of the 842 classified rows and was confident (one rule fired, text of 400 characters or fewer) for 302. Its confident guesses match the final pattern for 263 rows; over all rows it matches for 316. Most misses are magic weapons whose text opens with a flat bonus and goes on to a rider.
<!-- END FIRSTPASS -->

**Rules behaviour** means something the engine could resolve or track: a roll, a modifier, a state on
a Combatant, a resource, a change to the Map. An item whose text only says what it is, what it holds
or what it is used for has none and is excluded, with its reason.

**Needs nothing new** means the entry can be authored with the Effect component kinds, Item Property
kinds, Charges and Attunement that exist, with no missing kind, item mechanic or one-off. It does not
mean it runs today: only spell grants, Charges and Attunement run, and only for Homebrew items.

## The census

How to read it:

- **Rows** is how many seed rows the entry covers.
- **Use** says how the item acts: `worn` (passive while worn, held or carried), `action` (activated)
  or `consumed`.
- **Item Properties** are the builder rows and Design fields that would author the entry. Charges and
  Attunement are in **Flags**.
- **Effect kinds needed** lists every component kind, existing and missing.
- **Missing today** lists the missing component kinds, then `item:` mechanics, then any one-off
  mechanic no named kind covers. Each is defined below the table.
- **Flags**: `Ch` the item has Charges; `Ch*` it has a single limited use (once per dawn, ten uses in
  all) that fits the Charges model as it is; `At` it requires Attunement, as the seed flags it; `Co`
  it is used up; `Cu` it carries a curse.
- **Only grants a spell** is Yes when the entry does nothing but let its bearer cast the spells
  named. Whether those spells reach full is the spells census's question
  ([#237](https://github.com/JorisJonkers-dev/grimoire/issues/237)).

### Missing Effect component kinds

<!-- BEGIN MISSING -->
- `modifier`: A flat bonus or penalty while the Effect is active: to attack rolls, damage rolls, Armor Class, saving throws, ability checks, spell attack rolls, spell save DC, Initiative, Hit Point maximum or Proficiency Bonus. It can be scoped: attacks with this item only, ranged attacks only, one creature type, only while unarmored. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `Modifier`; today the engine has bonus dice (`bonus_die`) and a penalty from exhaustion, but no flat bonus.
- `resistance`: Resistance, Immunity or Vulnerability to one or more damage types, or ignoring a target's Resistance. The species and monster builders record resistances as data; no Effect grants one, and nothing read for this census applies one to damage in play.
- `damage`: Damage dice or a flat amount of one type dealt to one target outside an area save: an on-hit rider, the result of an attack roll, or automatic damage. `save_damage` only works on an Area behind a save, and `extra_damage` is dice the Effect's source adds against the bearer (Hunter's Mark), with no damage type.
- `trigger`: A typed event that fires components: a hit with this item, a natural 20 on the attack roll, taking damage, failing a save, entering or starting or ending a turn somewhere, dawn, or a fixed interval. `reacts` exists but is a reaction the DM resolves from free text. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `Trigger`.
- `save_gate`: A saving throw with its own DC source: a fixed number, 8 + an ability modifier + Proficiency Bonus, or the wielder's spell save DC, for one target or an Area. Today `area_save`, `save_damage` and `save_condition` always take the caster token's spell save DC (13 when the stat block has none) and need an Area. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `SaveGate`.
- `attack_gate`: An attack roll the Effect makes itself, with a fixed bonus or the wielder's ability modifier and Proficiency Bonus, gating the components behind it. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `AttackGate`.
- `heal`: Restores Hit Points: dice and a bonus, once or on a schedule. `temp_hp` exists; healing does not. Play heals for `potion-of-healing` through a rule keyed on the slug, not through an Effect. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `Heal`.
- `apply_condition`: Puts a condition on a target for a duration without an area save: on the user (Invisible), on a hit, or behind a save_gate or attack_gate. `save_condition` only works on an Area behind the caster's save DC. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `ApplyCondition`.
- `sense`: Grants a Sense with a range: Darkvision, Truesight, Blindsight. Tokens carry Senses and the item builder has a `sense` row, but no Effect grants one. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `Sense`.
- `speed`: Grants or changes a Speed: a Fly, Swim or Climb Speed of a number of feet or equal to the bearer's Speed, a doubled Speed, a floor, or added feet. `speed_penalty` only subtracts.
- `ability_score`: Sets an ability score to a floor (19, 21 to 29) or raises it with a cap, while worn or permanently.
- `check_edge`: Advantage or Disadvantage on ability checks of one skill or ability, or on Initiative, including Disadvantage imposed on other creatures' checks against the bearer. `edge` covers attack rolls and `save_edge` saving throws; nothing covers ability checks. The builder's Skill Boost row has no Effect behind it.
- `edge_scope`: A filter on `edge`, `save_edge` and `bonus_die`: only against one creature type, only against spells and other magical effects, only saves to avoid or end one condition, only ranged, spell or opportunity attacks, only with other weapons. Today an edge applies to every attack and a save_edge to every save of one ability.
- `proficiency`: Grants proficiency or Expertise with a weapon, an armor category, a skill or a tool, or a language the bearer knows.
- `immunity`: Immunity to a condition, or to a named hazard or class of effect: Poisoned, magical Paralysis or Restraint, falling damage, Difficult Terrain, suffocating under water, extreme cold, Divination targeting, thought reading.
- `outcome_change`: Changes the result of a d20 roll after it is known: a failed save becomes a success, a failed D20 Test is rerolled, a Death Saving Throw of 9 or lower counts as 10, a Critical Hit becomes a normal hit.
- `damage_reduction`: Reduces damage the bearer is about to take by dice or an amount, usually as a Reaction or against one damage type.
- `create_object`: Places an object, structure, barrier or vehicle with its own statistics (AC, Hit Points, size, what it blocks) on the Map or in an Inventory. ARCHITECTURE.md §9.3 names it `CreateObject`.
- `banish`: Removes a creature from the Map into another plane or an extradimensional space until it is released, returns or escapes.
- `compel`: Forces behaviour on a creature: it must flee, must attack the nearest creature, obeys the user's commands, or the user decides its move and action.
- `slay`: The target dies or is destroyed outright, usually behind a save or below a Hit Point threshold.
- `escape_check`: The Effect ends when the target, or a creature helping it, takes an action and succeeds on an ability check against a set DC, or when the binding object (AC, Hit Points) is destroyed. A Duration today can only repeat a save.
- `spell_store`: Stores the levels of a spell cast into the item, to be cast later by the bearer or turned into spell slots.
- `reflect_spell`: Absorbs a spell that targets only the bearer, or turns it back on its caster. `counter` only cancels a spell cast within a range.
- `stat_override`: Overrides a base statistic of the item or its bearer: a weapon's damage type or properties (Thrown, Finesse, returns to the hand), the Unarmed Strike die, an armor's Stealth Disadvantage or Strength requirement, the base Armor Class formula.
- `roll_table`: Rolls a die and resolves the matching row's components, including a flat percentage chance. Roll Table results can already own Effects (`roll_table_result`), but no component rolls on a table from inside an Effect.
- `stabilize`: Makes a creature at 0 Hit Points Stable without a check.
- `note`: A described outcome with nothing to compute, shown to the table and written to the Action Log without waiting on the DM. A `manual` component blocks on a DM prompt and makes the entry less than full, so descriptive outcomes need a kind of their own, or a ruling that they are not Manual Effects.
<!-- END MISSING -->

### Missing item mechanics

These are not Effect components. They are fields or rules the item side lacks.

<!-- BEGIN GAPS -->
- **spell-options**: A granted spell with options the builder's `spell` row lacks: a fixed save DC or attack bonus, a fixed spell level, extra Charges to cast at a higher level, a restriction on targets or modes (self only, Beasts only), its own duration or no Concentration, or a different casting time. Play only checks the grant and spends the Charges; the spell then resolves with the caster's own numbers.
- **use-limits**: Several independent limited uses on one item, or a recharge schedule other than dawn, a Long Rest or a Short Rest: after 1 hour, after a number of days, at dusk, a rolled number of hours, or a running total of minutes. An item holds one Charges pool today.
- **depletion**: What happens when the last charge or use is spent: roll 1d20 and destroy the item on a 1, the item becomes nonmagical, a lifetime total, or a rolled number of starting Charges.
- **variant**: A choice fixed when the Item Instance is made: its damage type, creature type, plane, colour, size or spell. Several seed rows bundle all their variants in one description.
- **curse-lock**: A curse the rules enforce: the item cannot be unattuned or removed until Remove Curse, and the curse stays hidden until the item is identified or attuned. The builder's `curse` row is text with a cannot-drop flag that play does not read.
- **utilize-check**: An item action that calls for an ability check against a set DC and reports the outcome, as tools do with Utilize.
- **charge-scaling**: An Effect that scales with the number of Charges spent on one use (1 to 3 Charges for more damage). Scaling today follows a spell slot, a level or a class table column.
- **focus**: The item counts as a Spellcasting Focus for some classes, or supplies free Material Components.
<!-- END GAPS -->

### One-off mechanics

A one-off is a rule only that entry has and that no kind above covers. Each is quoted in its row. A
one-off is a gap like any other, not a reason for the Manual exception list.

### Proposed Manual exceptions

An exception is proposed only where the outcome is the DM's judgment by nature. Each of these is
partial: the engine still resolves the parts with numbers.

<!-- BEGIN EXCEPTIONS -->
- **Bag of Beans.** For the planted-bean table only: most rows end in "GM's choice" (what the geyser spouts, which monster the toads become, what waits in the pyramid, where the beanstalk leads). The explosion is ordinary area damage and automates.
- **Dragon Orb.** For two parts: what the orb's dragon essence wants (it casts Suggestion on a Charmed user toward ends "the GM decides") and Call Dragons, whose result is which dragons arrive and how they feel about it. The saving throw, the Charmed condition, the Charges and the spells automate.
- **Marvelous Pigments.** The user paints "any number of three-dimensional objects and terrain features" into being. What was painted, what it is worth and what it does is open by nature; the engine can spend the pot and nothing more.
- **Mysterious Deck.** For seven cards: Fates, Flames, Rogue, Ruin, Sage, Void and the keep that Throne grants change the world or the story in ways only the DM can rule. The draw itself and the cards with numbers (Balance, Comet, Euryale, Fool, Gem, Jester, Knight, Moon, Puzzle, Skull, Star, Talons, the rest of Throne) automate; Key and Sun need the DM to pick an item, which is a choice and not a ruling.
<!-- END EXCEPTIONS -->

Considered and not proposed, because they are hard and not open:

- **Wand of Wonder.** Every row of its table is a spell, a condition, a summon or a harmless oddity.
  It needs `roll_table`, not a DM.
- **Efreeti Bottle, Bag of Tricks, Deck of Illusions, Robe of Useful Items.** A roll picks a creature
  or an object from a fixed list.
- **Vorpal Sword.** The clause "if the GM decides that the creature is too big" is a yes or no about a
  monster, answerable once per stat block.
- **Well of Many Worlds, Amulet of the Planes, Cubic Gate, Rod of Security.** The DM picks a
  destination or the dice do; what the engine resolves is who leaves the Map. That is the same
  question Plane Shift and Gate raise in the spells census.
- **Luck Blade, Ring of Three Wishes, Efreeti Bottle.** They grant Wish. Whether Wish is an exception
  is for the spells census to rule; the items only spend a charge.
- **Manual of Golems, the Manuals and Tomes.** Downtime with a cost and a fixed result.
- **Sphere of Annihilation, Mirror of Life Trapping, Iron Flask, Bag of Devouring.** Unusual, fully
  specified.
- **Gear that gives Advantage "where it applies"** (Crowbar, Book, Costume). Whether it applies is a
  switch the player sets, as with any situational Advantage.

A separate decision hides in the `note` kind. The entries that list it have a part that is a
described fact with nothing to compute (a vessel that cannot be moved, a beard that grows, telepathy
with an owl). If such a part must be a `manual` component, none of those entries can reach full and
each would need an exception. The census assumes a described outcome is not a Manual Effect and counts `note` as
a missing kind so the cost of that assumption is visible.

## Seed observations

Found while reading; they affect how SRD items are authored, and none is fixed here.

- **Five magic items are not flagged magic:** Ioun Stone, Potion of Giant Strength, Potion of
  Healing, Potions of Healing and Spell Scroll. The Airship is flagged nonmagical too and is excluded
  as a vehicle.
- **Several rows bundle variants in one description:** Ioun Stone (fourteen stones), Potion of Giant
  Strength (five), Potions of Healing (four), Carpet of Flying (four sizes), Horn of Valhalla (four),
  Ring of Elemental Command (four planes), Elemental Gem (four), Bag of Tricks (three), the Ivory
  Goats figurine (three goats), Dragon Scale Mail (ten dragons), Necklace of Prayer Beads (six beads)
  and the items that roll a damage type (Armor of Resistance, Ring of Resistance, Potion of
  Resistance). Ammunition of Slaying and the Belts of Giant Strength are split into one row per
  variant. The `variant` item mechanic stands for this.
- **Healing potions.** `inventory.Healing` knows four slugs; the seed has only `potion-of-healing`.
  The greater, superior and supreme potions are a table inside `potions-of-healing`.
- **Text defects:** `wand-of-web` carries the Wand of Fear description; `wand-of-fear` lacks its
  Regaining Charges paragraph; the Ivory Goats row includes the Golden Lions paragraph; the
  Mysterious Deck lists Donjon in its table without a paragraph for it; the Obsidian Steed's slug is
  spelled `-obsidian-stead`; two Holy Symbol names end in a stray `)`; Antitoxin ends in a stray
  "abo"; the Glass Bottle "holds up to 1 0.5 pints".
- **Attunement.** The `At` flag is the seed's. The Ioun Stone row is flagged as needing none while its
  own text speaks of Attunement to a stone ending.
- **Inline stat blocks.** The Avatar of Death (Mysterious Deck) exists only as text in the item; it is
  not a seeded monster. Every other creature an item summons is in the seed.

## Not verified

- The seed was not compared with the published SRD 5.2. Where a row is visibly wrong it is noted
  above and classified by what the item plainly is; `wand-of-web` is classified by its name.
- Whether each granted spell can itself reach full. That belongs to the spells census.
- Whether spell slots could be addressed by `resource_change`. It names a Resource, and nothing read
  here treats a spell slot as one, so the Pearl of Power is marked as a one-off.
- How the existing kinds behave in play beyond what their Go types and comments say. No test was run
  and the app was not started; this is a reading of the code and the data.
- The names of the missing kinds are proposals. The spells, Features and monsters censuses may name
  the same gap differently; the definitions above are what should be matched, not the names.
